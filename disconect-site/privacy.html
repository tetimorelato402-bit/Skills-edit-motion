Privacy

What Disconect keeps about you, and when.

At the door

When you scan a host’s door code you give your name and phone number. The host sees that you joined their night.

During the night

Your photos and videos go to that event’s feed and stories only. Nothing posts publicly and nothing gets a caption.

After the night

Guests of that event can view the collection and, after the official end time, download it.
