Does the app pause calls?

No. Calls, texts, WhatsApp, Uber and Lyft stay open inside the venue.

When can I download the photos?

After the official end time. Until then, view it in the feed.

Does the app post anything publicly?

No. Every shot stays in the event's own feed.

What if I open the app before the night starts?

The app switches to the camera by itself when the night begins.
