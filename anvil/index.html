How a week works.

You and two to five friends. Everyone says what they’ll do, and the app keeps score so nobody has to be the one who asks.

  1. Pick the thing and the days.

    Gym three times a week. A run on Sunday. Whatever you keep saying you’ll start.

  2. Go there.

    Anvil knows the places you set. When you arrive, the camera unlocks and you take a photo, right there.

  3. Your circle sees it.

    Your friends see your week fill in, one check-in at a time. You see theirs.

  4. Miss a day, and they notice.

    That’s the part that works. It’s a lot harder to skip when three people will see the gap.

Three things the app won’t bend on

You can’t fake being there.

The camera only opens at the place you picked. There’s no upload button and no camera roll, so last month’s gym photo doesn’t count.

You can’t excuse yourself.

Sick, travelling, a flat tyre: it happens. Ask, and your circle decides whether the day counts. One excused day a month.

You can’t quietly quit.

Go quiet and a friend can nudge you, and the rest of the circle sees it. Nobody outside your circle ever does.

Your friends see that you went. Not where you are.

Anvil uses your location for one job: noticing when you arrive at a place you chose. It keeps the places you set and when you arrived there, not a trail of where you go.

The details are in our privacy policy, written to be read.

Your circle sees

  • That you checked in, and on which day
  • The photo you took there
  • Your week and your streak
  • For runs, the route line and distance, with no map under it

Nobody ever sees

  • Where you are, or a map of it
  • How far away you are
  • The exact time you arrived
  • Your camera roll, contacts or other apps

Fair questions.

The things people usually ask before they invite anyone.

Do my friends have to pay?

No. Joining someone’s circle is always free. Only the person who runs the circle pays, and only if they want more than one circle or more than three people in it.

Why does it ask for location “Always”?

So it can notice when you arrive at a place you chose, even with the app closed, and unlock the camera there. That’s the only thing it’s used for.

Nobody in your circle ever sees your location, a map, or how far away you are. You can switch location off in iPhone Settings at any time; the camera just won’t unlock at your places until you turn it back on.

What if I was there and it didn’t notice?

GPS isn’t perfect, and a missed check-in isn’t proof you weren’t there. Tell your circle and ask them to excuse the day. They decide, not the app.

Isn’t being nudged in front of everyone a bit much?

It’s a tap on the shoulder from a friend, seen only by the few people in your circle. Nothing is posted anywhere public, and there’s no feed for strangers. Nudges are for encouraging each other; using them to pressure or shame someone is against our rules.

Can I leave a circle?

Yes. Ask to leave and the circle can let you go straight away. If they don’t, you’re out on your own after 7 days. You can block or report anyone at any time.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Anvil is iPhone only for now, which means everyone in a circle needs an iPhone too.

How do I cancel?

In the app (tap your photo, then Plan), in iPhone Settings under Subscriptions if you paid through Apple, or on our pause or cancel page. No calls, no forms, no reasons needed. Cancel during the trial and you’re never charged.

Who can use Anvil?

Anyone 13 or older. If you’re under 18, you need a parent or guardian’s permission. Circles are always private.
