Cancel with Apple

  1. Open iPhone Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Anvil.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.
Apple's instructions

What happens when you cancel

  • You won't be charged again. You'll get an email confirming it.
  • You keep paid features until the end of the period you already paid for. After that, you move to the free plan.
  • Cancelled during a free trial? You won't be charged at all.
  • Your circles, commitments and history stay. [Photos older than 3 months stay saved for 90 days so you can resubscribe or keep them.]
  • Payments aren't refunded for partly used periods, except where the law requires. Charged by mistake? Email us and we'll make it right.

Want to delete everything instead?

Cancelling keeps your account. To delete your account and your data, open the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. If you pay through Apple, also cancel there, because deleting your account doesn't stop Apple's billing.

Trouble cancelling?

Email [support@yourdomain] with the subject "Cancel" from the email on your account. We'll cancel within 1 business day and confirm by email.
