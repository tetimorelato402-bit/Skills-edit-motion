Cancel your plan.

Where did you subscribe?

  1. On the web
  2. In the app Your photo→Plan→Cancel
  3. With Apple Settings→Your name→Subscriptions→Anvil→Cancel Apple’s help

What happens next

  • No more charges. We email you to confirm.
  • You keep Anvil until the period you paid for ends.
  • Cancelled during your free trial? You’re never charged.
  • Your circles and history stay saved if you come back.
  • Partly used periods aren’t refunded, except where the law requires. Charged by mistake? Email us and we’ll fix it.

Want your data gone too? In the app: Settings → Delete account. If you pay through Apple, cancel there as well; deleting your account doesn’t stop Apple’s billing.

Stuck? Email with the subject “Cancel”. We cancel within 1 business day and confirm by email.

Pause instead?

Skip a month. No charge. Your circle, streaks and photos stay put.

Billing restarts in 1 month. We’ll remind you 3 days before.
