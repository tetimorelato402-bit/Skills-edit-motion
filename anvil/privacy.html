Privacy policy

What Anvil sees, and what it never shows.

Last updated October 8, 2026

Draft for legal review. Items in [brackets] are still to be confirmed.
The short version
  • We use your location for one job: knowing when you arrive at the places you chose, so the camera can unlock.
  • Your circle sees that you showed up. Never where you are, how far away you are, or the exact time you arrived.
  • Photos are taken live in the app. We never open your camera roll.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your photos to train AI.
  • You can delete your account and your data from the app at any time.

Who we are

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your personal information, or "share" it for cross-context advertising.
  • Show ads in Anvil, or let advertisers track you.
  • Show anyone your location, a map of where you are, or your exact arrival time.
  • Read your camera roll, contacts or other apps.
  • Use your photos, voice or location to train AI models.
  • Rank or recommend your content to strangers. There is no public feed; circles are private.

Who we share it with

  • Your circle, only what the table above says they can see.
  • Service providers that run Anvil for us, under contracts that limit their use of your data: cloud hosting and storage [provider], payments (Stripe, Apple), notifications (Apple Push Notification service), email [provider], crash reporting [provider].
  • The law, when we must answer a valid legal request, or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer, if Anvil is sold or merged. This policy would continue to apply to your data.

Teens (ages 13 to 17)

  • Anvil is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we delete the account and its data.
  • Anyone under 18 needs permission from a parent or guardian to use Anvil.
  • Parents and guardians can use Anvil's parental controls to [link to their teen's account, see and manage the teen's circles, and turn off features such as heads-ups].
  • Teen accounts are never public, and we never show ads to anyone.
  • A parent or guardian can ask us to delete their teen's account at [privacy@yourdomain].

Your choices

  • Location: change it anytime in iPhone Settings. If you turn it off, the camera can't unlock at your places.
  • Notifications and heads-ups: turn them off in the app's Settings.
  • Photos: delete any photo in Memories.
  • Your data: ask for a copy, correct it, or delete it. Delete your account in the app (Settings, Delete account) or email [privacy@yourdomain]. We delete your data within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep. Your circle keeps its weekly history, shown without your name.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out. We honor these requests for everyone. We don't sell or share data for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, limit who on our team can access it, and use providers with strong security practices. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Where your data is kept

On servers in the United States. If you use Anvil from another country, your data is transferred to the US.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
